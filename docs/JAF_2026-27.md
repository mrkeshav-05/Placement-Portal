# IIIT Lucknow --- Student-Facing Job Announcement Form (JAF) 2026--27

## Field Reference for AI Agents

This document defines the fields from the IIIT Lucknow Training &
Placement Cell (T&P Cell) JAF that may be shown to students. Use it as
context when building student-facing job listings, job-detail pages,
APIs, exports, and AI-generated summaries.

**Source:** `JAF IIIT Lucknow 2026-27(3).docx`\
**Form owner:** Training & Placement Cell, Indian Institute of
Information Technology Lucknow\
**Placement Cell email:** placements@iiitl.ac.in\
**Website:** https://placements.iiitl.ac.in/

> This is a student-facing projection of the original JAF. It
> intentionally omits recruiter/HR contact details, the organization's
> postal address, tentative number of hires, and medical requirements.
> Those fields must not appear in student-facing interfaces or outputs.

## 7. Fields Excluded from Student-Facing JAF

The following source-JAF fields are internal / not required for the
student-facing listing. Do not expose them in student-facing pages,
APIs, exports, notifications, downloadable documents, or AI-generated
summaries:

-   Recruiter / contact person's name.
-   Recruiter / contact person's designation.
-   Recruiter / contact person's email address.
-   Recruiter / contact person's mobile number.
-   Recruiter / contact person's landline.
-   Recruiter / contact person's office address.
-   Organization's postal address.
-   Tentative number of hires.
-   Medical requirements and medical-test details.

The exclusion of medical requirements applies to this student-facing JAF
projection. Do not display medical-test details from the source form in
the student-facing listing.

Do not remove the **HR Interview** selection round. It is a
recruitment-process detail, not a recruiter contact field.

## 8. AI Agent: Student-Facing Data Capture Checklist

When extracting or presenting a JAF, use these sections:

1.  **Organization Overview**
    -   Organization name
    -   Organization type
    -   Website
    -   Nature of business / industry sector
2.  **Job Profile**
    -   Opportunity type
    -   Internship duration, if applicable
    -   Designation
    -   Job description
    -   Tentative joining date
    -   Tentative job location
    -   Work mode
3.  **Eligibility**
    -   Yes / No eligibility response for each programme and branch
        combination in the JAF
    -   Discipline codes, as defined in the form
4.  **Internship Details**
    -   Monthly stipend in INR
    -   Duration
    -   PPO offered: Yes / No
    -   Expected PPO CTC in LPA, if applicable
5.  **Compensation**
    -   For each degree category (B.Tech., M.Tech., M.Sc., MBA, Ph.D.):
        fixed, variable, and CTC in yearly INR
    -   Service bond / contract: Yes / No
    -   Bond duration, amount, and conditions, if applicable
    -   Additional benefits
    -   Detailed salary / perks breakup
6.  **Selection Procedure**
    -   Minimum CGPA
    -   Backlogs allowed: Yes / No
    -   Pre-Placement Talk: Yes / No
    -   Resume shortlisting: Yes / No
    -   Resume-shortlisting criteria, if applicable
    -   For each listed selection round (Aptitude Test, Technical Test,
        Technical Interview, HR Interview): whether conducted, duration,
        number of rounds, and sequence
    -   Optional additional selection-process details

### Data-handling rules

-   Do not assume a blank field means "No"; represent it as **not
    provided** until confirmed.
-   Keep stipend distinct from salary / CTC.
-   Keep PPO offered distinct from a confirmed full-time offer.
-   Preserve units: stipend in **INR per month**, compensation in
    **yearly INR**, and expected PPO CTC in **LPA**.
-   Preserve the distinction between tentative joining date / job
    location and confirmed details.
-   Do not infer eligibility for an unlisted programme or branch.
-   For conditional fields (for example, bond terms when a bond exists),
    capture only the supplied details.
-   Do not expose any field listed in Section 7, even if it exists in
    the internal JAF record.
-   The source JAF does not specify a data schema, mandatory-field
    rules, or validation constraints. Do not invent them.
